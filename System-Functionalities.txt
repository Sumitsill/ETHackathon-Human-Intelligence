================================================================================
        ET UNIFIED OPERATIONS BRAIN 2026 - WHAT THE SYSTEM CAN DO
================================================================================
Updated: 2026-07-20
File: SYSTEM_FUNCTIONALITIES_SIMPLE.txt

Here is a clear, simple guide explaining everything this application can do 
right now across its frontend dashboard and AI backend services.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. READING DOCUMENTS & BUILDING A KNOWLEDGE GRAPH (Module 1)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
* Reads Any File Format: Upload PDFs (OEM manuals, SOPs), blueprints/images, 
  Excel spreadsheets, or Gmail inbox emails.
* Smart OCR & Text Reading: Automatically reads scanned paper documents and 
  handwriting using vision AI to convert raw files into searchable text.
* Interactive Knowledge Graph: Extracts equipment tags (e.g. Pump P-204, 
  Valve V-102), process limits, and safety standards, connecting them into 
  a visual network graph.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. GROUNDED AI EXPERT COPILOT (Module 2)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
* Verified Answers with Sources: Ask questions about refinery equipment, 
  operating limits, or safety rules. Every answer includes exact citations 
  pointing back to the original source document.
* Field Technician Mode: Mobile workers in the plant can turn on 
  "Field Tech Mode" to receive lightweight, fast text answers optimized 
  for low-bandwidth phones.
* Knowledge Decay Warnings: Automatically warns engineers if an answer is 
  based on a document or SOP that is over 12 months old and needs re-verification.
* Visual Diagrams: Automatically generates interactive Mindmaps and process 
  Flowcharts directly from written operating manuals.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. FAILURE DIAGNOSIS & MAINTENANCE INTELLIGENCE (Module 3 - MIRA)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
* 5-Why Failure Analysis: Builds interactive 5-Why root cause trees when 
  equipment breaks down (such as pump seizures, seal leaks, or compressor trips).
* Predictive Anomaly Alerts: Scans live machine sensors (vibration, heat, 
  pressure) to flag abnormal behavior before machines fail.
* Near-Miss Pattern Mining: Analyzes historical work orders to discover 
  recurring failure signatures across different plant machines.
* Smart Maintenance Scheduler: Optimizes labor hours and budget limits to 
  schedule maintenance work orders based on safety urgency.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4. SAFETY & REGULATORY COMPLIANCE INTELLIGENCE (Module 4 - QRCI)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
* Automatic Law & Rule Scans: Scans plant operations against government and 
  industry safety standards including:
  - Factories Act (Safety fencing, work permits)
  - OISD (Oil Industry Safety Directorate regulations)
  - PESO (Petroleum and Explosives Safety Organization rules)
  - IBR (Indian Boiler Regulations)
* Audit-Ready Evidence Compiler: Assembles complete audit evidence packages 
  combining asset records, work orders, inspection logs, and statutory citations 
  with one click.
* Audit Auto-Narration: Generates plain-language executive cover summaries 
  on audit packages so non-technical government auditors can easily review them.
* Regulatory Diff Alerts: Pushes alerts when government regulations change, 
  showing exactly which machines are affected and what actions engineers must take.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
5. BRAIN SYNC - UNIFIED OPERATIONAL & COMPLIANCE RISK TRACKING
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
* Cross-Module Risk Sync: Bridges maintenance failure alerts with safety 
  compliance records. If a pump experiences a mechanical failure, Brain Sync 
  immediately checks and alerts if that failure also breaches government 
  safety regulations.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
6. WEB DASHBOARD & EASY SERVER LAUNCH
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
* Operations Command Center: A modern web interface (running on Next.js) for 
  viewing plant health metrics, chatting with the copilot, inspecting 5-Why 
  trees, and approving compliance reports.
* Single-Command Server Launcher: Running `python run_servers.py` launches 
  all 4 backend AI microservices concurrently on ports 8000, 8001, 8002, and 8003.
